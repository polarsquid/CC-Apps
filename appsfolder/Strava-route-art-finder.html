Pick a suburb and a distance. It plans loops from your start that cover every street, lane and path in it, and keeps a map of the ones you've done.

Runs you've done

    Shapes

    Tap shapes to switch them on or off. Only switched-on shapes are searched.

    Every street counts the streets, lanes, footpaths, park paths and public driveways inside the suburb boundary. Footpaths running right beside a road count as part of that road. A street is done when a run you've ticked off goes along it. How a route is judged: the shape is dropped onto the map, every corner is routed along runnable streets and paths, then the finished loop is compared with the ideal outline (overlap of the two silhouettes, average drift, and whether sharp points like ears, fins and star tips were actually reached). Routes that drift, cut corners off, or double back on themselves are thrown out.
